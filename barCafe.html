BAR

At The Gochukha Bar, immerse yourself in an exquisite experience of fine beverages and warm Bhutanese hospitality. Savor our expertly crafted cocktails, premium spirits, and a selection of local brews, all served in a serene, elegant setting. Unwind with signature drinks that celebrate the essence of Bhutan, making every sip a memorable journey.
