THE GOCHUKHA

A Legacy of Elegance and Bhutanese Charm

Nestled in the heart of Thimphu, the capital city of Bhutan, The Gochukha blends traditional charm with modern luxury. More than a hotel, it’s a gateway to authentic cultural experiences and exceptional hospitality, creating memories that last a lifetime.

THE GOCHUKHA DINING PHILOSOPHY

At the newly launched The Gochukha Hotel, we redefine dining with a personal touch. While we are yet to open a traditional restaurant, we offer exclusive experiences tailored to your preferences. Enjoy thoughtfully curated food hampers or have a private chef craft exceptional Bhutanese and international dishes just for you. Using fresh, locally sourced ingredients, we aim to deliver flavors that are as authentic as they are unforgettable. At The Gochukha, every meal is a celebration of taste and a promise of something truly unique.
